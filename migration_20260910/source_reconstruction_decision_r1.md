# Decision: do not run the proposed source-reconstruction pilot

2026-09-10. Read-only follow-up to `source_reconstruction_prior_art_r1.md`. This note withdraws that note's experiment suggestion in its current form; it does not change the literature findings, frozen DEV107 run, existing results, or any paper method. No preregistration, implementation, GPU run, benchmark QA inspection, or promotion was performed.

**Additional reconstruction scoring over the existing candidate population would test retrieval visibility, not recover omitted source information.** The present design does not isolate the claimed scientific difference, so another pilot is not warranted.

The frozen source-only `protocol` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_results_r1/collected/run/protocol.json:66`) contains both complete source histories and their Seed memories. A fresh local check, without any benchmark QA, compared each original turn ID and complete body with individual `dialogue_pair` entries that cite that same ID:

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| LoCoMo conv-49 | 509 | 509 | 305 + 509 = 814 |
| LongMemEval DEV12 d6062bb9 | 532 | 532 | 725 + 532 = 1,257 |

There were zero uncovered source IDs. The relevant Seed arrays begin at `protocol line 5967` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_results_r1/collected/run/protocol.json:5967`) and `line 48272` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_results_r1/collected/run/protocol.json:48272`). These findings apply only to the two inspected histories, not to all DEV107 or all benchmark histories. “Stored” does not imply the reader successfully retrieves or uses that information.

The candidate construction confirms the distinction. ``minimum_parent_cover()`` (historical source: `migration_20260910/paper_lme_partition_adapter/source/parent_evidence.py:8`) finds existing Seed units by source-ID coverage; `line 37` (historical source: `migration_20260910/paper_lme_partition_adapter/source/parent_evidence.py:37`) concatenates their existing text. ``make_options()`` (historical source: `migration_20260910/paper_lme_partition_adapter/source/parent_evidence.py:42`) uses those payloads with Q0 retrieval cues. The pilot filters mapped single/full options at `pilot.py:127` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_pilot_r1/pilot.py:127`) and appends selected aliases to the unchanged Seed at `pilot.py:391` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_pilot_r1/pilot.py:391`). The planned populations were 33 options in 16 groups for conv-49 and 36 in 16 groups for d6062bb9. None introduces source text unavailable in Seed.

Four issues prevent the earlier proposed comparison from identifying additional information preservation:

- **Target split:** Existing QB is a different wording of a subset of fit probe IDs, with the answer held fixed; it is not held-out source information (`pilot.py:87` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_pilot_r1/pilot.py:87`), `source_views_v2_pilot.py:81` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_pilot_r1/source_views_v2_pilot.py:81`)). Replacing this with a hash split of source reconstruction targets withholds their scores but leaves every target's original text available in constructor input and Seed. That would be an objective holdout, not evidence that previously omitted facts were retained.
- **Shared budget:** The existing comparison caps additional storage at 2,000 tokens and reader evidence at 2,048; it does not impose a common compressed-memory budget on the whole Seed (`README:15` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_pilot_r1/README.md:15`), `README:20` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_pilot_r1/README.md:20`)). Gains can therefore result from routing, repetition, order, or packing. Restricting the entire memory to a new budget would define a different compression experiment, requiring its own justification.
- **Copy control:** Labeling targets “copied” or “not copied” after each arm's packing is treatment-dependent and does not hold lexical evidence constant across arms. Whole-turn reconstruction particularly rewards verbatim source availability. Removing the original target text from every candidate would change the task again and does not by itself establish semantic preservation. The current scorer has no independent semantic-information attribution measure.
- **Identical-input variation:** ``Scorer.score()`` (historical source: `migration_20260910/paper_lme_partition_adapter/source/run_evidence_utility.py:30`) queues duplicate uncached user/answer keys before cache writes, allowing separate native evaluations. Audit and QB submit before/after jobs together (`pilot.py:395` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_pilot_r1/pilot.py:395`), `pilot.py:441` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_pilot_r1/pilot.py:441`)). The completed `report:10` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_results_r1/FINAL_SOURCE_PILOT_REPORT.json:10`) records LME audit gain +0.0004155367 despite all four inputs being identical; an identical-input LoCoMo QB pair differs by -0.0060913563 (`diagnosis:198` (historical source: `migration_20260910/packed_marginal_results_r1/SOURCE_PILOT_DIAGNOSIS.json:198`)). Canonicalizing exact inputs before scoring can prevent false attribution, but cannot turn a routing experiment into information-retention evidence. The observed differences are not a universal noise bound, and existing gates/results must not be corrected retroactively.

**Decision rule for this design audit:** reject launching the proposed pilot when all source information is already present, additions only remap existing payloads, and the proposed target/copy controls cannot separate preservation from exposure. All three conditions hold here. No extra NLL run is needed to establish them. A future retention hypothesis would need a justified representation that actually loses or preserves different source information and controls that keep direct target exposure comparable; this note does not preregister such a replacement.

Continue the unchanged frozen DEV107 transfer evaluation. Neither this audit nor a source reconstruction metric substitutes for official LongMemEval-S `evaluate_qa.py` and `print_qa_metrics.py` or proves cross-model generalization.
