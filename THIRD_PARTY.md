# Third-party code and benchmark data

This is a scoped archive of research experiments. Existing copyright and license notices in vendored baseline snapshots remain in force. No new blanket license or relicensing is applied to third-party material.

- LoCoMo scoring and historical HiGMem code: see `HiGMem/LICENSE` and licenses under `code/locomo_execution_archive/snapshots/`.
- LongMemEval official evaluation: see `migration_20260910/official_longmemeval/LICENSE` and `UPSTREAM_PROVENANCE.json`.
- Baseline implementations and adapters: refer to the adjacent LICENSE/NOTICE files in each source snapshot. Hash/provenance receipts identify archived revisions where available.
- Benchmark conversations, frozen experiment inputs, generated answers, and evaluation receipts retain their original provenance and applicable source terms. They are included as research inputs/evidence, not as newly authored content.

`MANIFEST.json` maps this export to its local source paths and SHA256 values. It is an inventory of preserved files, not a statement that every historical source or dependency has been recovered.
